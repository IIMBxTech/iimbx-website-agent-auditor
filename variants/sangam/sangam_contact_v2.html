FAQ

Common Questions

Are Sangam courses really free?
▼
Yes — 100% free for all higher-education faculty. Programs are funded by IIMBx and supported by IIM Bangalore with no hidden fees.
Who is eligible to join?
▼
All higher-education faculty, instructional designers, and EdTech professionals in India. Some programs are open to international educators.
Will I receive a certificate?
▼
Yes — upon successful completion you receive an IIMBx / IIM Bangalore certificate you can share on LinkedIn and your academic profile.
How can my institution partner with Sangam?
▼
We welcome institutional partnerships. Email sangam@iimb.ac.in with details about your institution and the collaboration you have in mind.
How much time per week do courses require?
▼
Self-paced courses require 3–5 hours per week. Cohort-based programs typically require 6–8 hours per week including live sessions.
