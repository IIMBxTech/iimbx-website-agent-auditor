WHO WE ARE

A Manufacturer Driven by Passion & Purpose

Krishitek Industries Pvt. Ltd. is a pioneering manufacturer of farm machinery and equipment that blends the art of agriculture with cutting-edge technology, thereby helping in sustainable farming. We're a company driven by a passion for innovation and a deep-rooted respect for the farming community.

Our name, Krishitek, embodies our ethos of blending the best of agriculture and technology to empower farmers to achieve better yields, higher productivity, and economic stability. With a comprehensive range of high-quality power reaper machines, power weeders, and mini tillers, we are dedicated to helping farmers achieve their full potential.

Since our inception in 2012, we have been at the forefront of the farm mechanisation revolution, introducing innovative products that address the needs of farmers. We manufacture all our products in India with a focus on quality, durability, and reliability, adhering to stringent quality standards.

Our farm machinery and equipment are designed to withstand the tough demands of the farming landscape, making us the go-to choice for farmers — not only in India but also in neighbouring countries and Africa. When you choose Krishitek, you're getting a partner who's committed to your success.
