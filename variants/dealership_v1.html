FAQs

Dealer FAQs

Everything you need to know before becoming a Krishitek dealer.

What is the minimum investment required to become a dealer?
The minimum investment varies by region and product mix. Our team will discuss the specific requirements with you during the onboarding call after your application is reviewed.
Do you provide exclusive territory rights?
Yes, we offer territory protection to authorized dealers, ensuring you are the preferred Krishitek partner in your designated area, subject to performance targets.
What kind of training and support do you provide?
We provide comprehensive product training for your sales and service team, co-branded marketing materials, social media support, and a dedicated relationship manager.
How long does the application process take?
Once you submit your application, our team will contact you within 24 hours. The full onboarding process typically takes 5–7 working days.
Can I become a dealer if I already sell other agricultural equipment?
Absolutely! Many of our dealers already have existing agri equipment businesses. Krishitek products complement most other farm equipment lines perfectly.
Is there a warranty policy for dealers to extend to customers?
Yes. All Krishitek products come with a manufacturer warranty. As an authorized dealer, you will have direct access to our warranty and service support system.
