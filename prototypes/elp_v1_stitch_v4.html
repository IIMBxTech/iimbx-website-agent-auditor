Frequently Asked Questions.

What is the difference between ELP and NAM?

ELP (Emerging Leaders Programme) is a longer, 8-month cohort program that covers core management disciplines (finance, strategy, customer metrics) in deeper academic detail with multiple on-campus sessions. NAM is a 6-month corporate-nomination program focused on practical leadership capabilities (coaching, change leadership) designed for cohort-based corporate learning pathways.

Are there on-campus sessions at IIM Bangalore?

Yes, ELP 2.0 includes three mandatory on-campus immersion sessions, including a final valedictory in Month 8, where you will present your Action Learning Project directly to faculty and sponsors.

What certificate is awarded?

Participants who successfully complete all course requirements and defend their Capstone project will be awarded the Professional Certificate in Emerging Leadership by IIM Bangalore / IIMBx.
