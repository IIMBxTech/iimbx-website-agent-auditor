Eight capabilities,
ranked by HR leaders.

Capabilities that the people who hire, develop and lose managers said matter most in 2026. Universal across organisation types.

01

Learning Agility

Unlearn and relearn fast. Speed plus innovation under ambiguity.

02

Emotional Intelligence

Navigate generational gaps, hybrid realities and diverse employee needs without flattening them.

03

Change Leadership

Own the transition. Role-model behaviour. Manage the very real fear of becoming invisible.

04

Digital Fluency & AI

Leverage tech tools. Translate customer complexity into solutions teams can build.

05

Purpose Clarity

Connect personal purpose to organisational north star. Make purpose observable in behaviour.

06

Delegation & Empowerment

Simplify approval processes. Remove redundant layers. Give real authority, not the illusion of it.

07

Critical Thinking

Double-click on issues. Run bold experiments inside safe pods. Decide with data, not vibes.

08

Coaching Leadership

Mentor. Give regular feedback. Build a "hi-touch + hi-tech" culture — not annual reviews.

Three archetypes. One manager.

The new-age manager wears three hats every week. NAM is engineered to develop all three in parallel across the six months.

C
Archetype 01

The Coach.

Builds people. Doesn't direct them. Replaces approval bottlenecks with feedback loops. Embeds coaching as a daily practice.

Δ
Archetype 02

The Change Enabler.

Owns transformation visibly. Role-models the behaviour the team is being asked to adopt. Manages the fear of becoming invisible.

⇄
Archetype 03

The Digital Translator.

Bridges technology and people. Translates customer complexity into solutions. Brings AI and analytics into the manager's working toolkit.
