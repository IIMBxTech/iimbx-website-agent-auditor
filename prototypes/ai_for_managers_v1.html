This programme is not designed to make you a data scientist; it is designed to make you a data-driven leader. We focus on the intersection of artificial intelligence and strategic management.

You will learn to identify high-impact AI opportunities, manage complex technical teams, navigate ethical considerations, and measure the true ROI of machine learning initiatives within your enterprise.

Pedagogy: Harvard Business Publishing case studies, peer discussions, practical assignments.
