Curriculum

Course Syllabus

Week 1

Mechanics of Financial Accounting

Fundamental terms, recording transactions, and financial accounting regulations.

Week 2

Reading Financial Statements

Balance sheets, income statements, and cash flow statement analysis.

Week 3

Financial Statement Analysis

Assessing health, asset management, and risk benchmarking.

Week 4

Product Costing

Job, customer, and process costing. Activity-based costing methods.

Week 5

Cost Analysis for Decision Making

Break-even analysis, optimal product mix, and operating leverage.

Week 6

Budgeting and Variance Analysis

Master budgets, strategic planning, and investigating variances.

Week 7

Financial Markets

Financial systems, instruments, and the role of the finance function.

Week 8

Capital Budgeting

Time value of money, estimation of project cash flows, and cost of capital.

Week 9

Working Capital Management

Receivables, inventory, cash management, and short-term financial planning.
