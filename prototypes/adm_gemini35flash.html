Curriculum

Course Syllabus

Week 1

Mechanics of Financial Accounting

Fundamental terms, recording transactions, and financial statements regulations.

Week 2

Reading Financial Statements

Understand the Balance Sheet, Income Statement, and Cash Flow Statement.

Week 3

Financial Statement Analysis

Assessing financial health, risk, asset management, and benchmarking.

Week 4

Product Costing

Job costing, process costing, indirect cost allocation, and activity-based costing.

Week 5

Cost Analysis for Decision Making

Break-even analysis, marginal costing, pricing, and make-or-buy decisions.

Week 6

Budgeting and Variance Analysis

Master budgets, strategic planning, standard setting, and overhead variances.

Week 7

Financial Markets

Financial systems, direct capital flow, agency cost, and the finance function.

Week 8

Capital Budgeting

Time value of money, cash flow estimation, and project budgeting techniques.

Week 9

Working Capital Management

Managing payables, receivables, inventory, and short-term planning.
