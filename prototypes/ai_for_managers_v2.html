What You Will Learn

Designed for the Manager Who Leads Change.

This programme is not designed to make you a data scientist. It is designed to make you a data-driven leader who can identify high-impact AI opportunities, manage technical teams, and measure the true ROI of machine learning initiatives.

Pedagogy drawn from Harvard Business Publishing case studies, live cohort sessions with IIM-B faculty, and practical assignments built on real business problems.

01
Strategic AI Leadership
Frame AI as a business strategy, not a technology project. Lead adoption across functions.
02
Data Fluency Without Code
Read, question, and challenge data outputs with the confidence of a practitioner.
03
Ethics & Governance
Navigate bias, fairness, and regulatory frameworks that will shape AI in the decade ahead.
04
Generative AI in Business
Move beyond ChatGPT use-cases to enterprise-grade LLM deployments and ROI measurement.
