This isn't a refresher course.
It's a re-wiring.

ELP is built for the moment between getting the title and earning it. Seven months of structured rigour to take you from leading work to leading people, capital, customers, strategy and AI-driven decisions — with one capstone that proves you can.

01

Lead with rigour, not instinct.

Frameworks from IIM Bangalore faculty for project leadership, communication, and influence — calibrated for first-time managers.

02

Read a balance sheet like an owner.

Valuation, corporate finance, sustainable value creation. Stop deferring to finance — sit with them as a peer.

03

Build customer value, end-to-end.

CRM and digital marketing taught by faculty who write the curriculum the market uses. Move from acquiring customers to compounding them.

04

Think strategically. Innovate operationally.

Advanced corporate strategy paired with innovation at the leadership level — the two skills most often missing in the manager who got promoted on execution.

05

Decide with data. Not with vibes.

A dedicated theme on data-driven decision making and Generative AI for managers. Use the tools as a leader uses them, not as the team uses them.

06

Ship something that matters.

The Action Learning Project capstone runs across the entire programme. You don't write a paper. You move a number inside your own organisation.

Capstone · Action Learning Project

Don't write a paper.
Move a number.

The Action Learning Project (ALP) runs across all seven months, not bolted on at the end. You pick a real problem from your own organisation, and the programme bends around it.

Faculty mentor you. Cohort peers stress-test your hypotheses. You ship a recommendation, a prototype, or a measurable change — and present it at the valedictory in Month 7.

Month 1

Define the problem.

Scope a real business question from your own desk. Faculty review the brief.

Month 3

In-campus stress test.

First on-campus immersion. Pitch the plan. Peers challenge it. Refine.

Month 5

Apply frameworks.

Themes 3 & 4 wrap. Your project is now informed by customer, strategy and innovation lenses.

Month 7

Ship & defend.

Valedictory on campus. Present to faculty and your sponsoring leader. Walk away with a result, not a binder.

AI & Digital Strategy

Built for managers about to step up.

If you were promoted in the last two years, or are about to be, ELP closes the gap between the work that got you here and the leadership the next chair demands.

Ideal for:

  • First-time managers who've inherited a team and are learning leadership on the job, often without scaffolding.
  • Emerging leaders (3 to 8 yrs) in product, operations, technology, marketing or finance being groomed for cross-functional ownership.
  • Founders and senior ICs moving into general-management roles who need rigorous breadth across leadership, finance, customers, strategy and AI.

You should apply if you have:

  • 3 to 8 years of professional experience, with at least one year managing people or owning a cross-functional outcome.
  • A graduate degree in any discipline, including engineering, management, science, design, or relevant fields.
  • A real problem at your current organisation that you'd like to make the focus of your Action Learning Project.
