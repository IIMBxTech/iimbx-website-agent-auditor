01. OVERVIEW

The Leadership Threshold

The transition from a high-performing individual contributor to a strategic leader is the most critical shift in a professional's career. It demands a fundamental rewiring of how you perceive value creation.

Emerging Leaders Programme 2.0 is meticulously designed to bridge this gap. We focus on discarding the tactical mindsets that earned you your promotion, replacing them with the strategic frameworks necessary to guide teams, navigate ambiguity, and drive organizational impact.

04. SUPPORT

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the time commitment required?

expand_more
Participants should expect to dedicate approximately 4-6 hours per week. This includes live virtual sessions, self-paced modules, and practical assignments applied directly to your current role.

Do I need to be a manager currently to apply?

expand_more
No. While the programme is ideal for current first-time managers, it is equally valuable for senior individual contributors who are being groomed for managerial positions in the near future.

Is there an in-person component?

expand_more
No, the 7-month programme is delivered entirely via a blended online format to accommodate working professionals globally.
