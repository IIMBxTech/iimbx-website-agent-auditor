Programme Overview

Built for managers, not accountants.

Accounting information drives every business decision — yet most managers never received a structured grounding in it. This course changes that. Over nine weeks you build the confidence to read financial statements, challenge cost reports, and communicate with finance teams as a peer.

Each week adds a layer — from recording transactions through to capital budgeting and working capital management. No prior finance background required.

Week by Week

Course Syllabus

Nine weeks. Each builds on the last. By Week 9 you can run a full financial analysis end-to-end.

Fundamental accounting terms · Recording transactions · Reading financial statements · Accounting regulations · Annual report structure · Uses of financial data
Balance sheet — shareholders' funds, liabilities, assets · Income statement — revenue, expenses, profit · Cash flow statements explained
Assessing financial health · Asset management ratios · Cost management · Leverage and risk · Benchmarking against industry peers
Job costing · Process costing · Customer costing · Indirect cost allocation · Misallocation pitfalls · Activity-based costing for precision
Absorption vs marginal costing · Break-even analysis · Pricing decisions · Make-or-buy decisions · Optimal product mix · Operating leverage
Strategic planning and budgets · Master budget preparation · Setting standards · Overhead, material, and sales mix variance analysis
Financial system structure · Institutional and direct capital flow · Financial instruments · Role and structure of the finance function · Agency cost
Time value of money · Capital budgeting techniques · Project cash flow estimation · Cost of capital · Capital structure decisions
Short-term financial planning · Working capital cycle · Financing strategies · Managing inventory, receivables, payables, and cash
