What You Will Learn

Designed for the Manager Who Leads Change.

Not a data science course. A leadership course built on data science. You will learn to identify high-impact AI opportunities, govern technical teams, and measure the real ROI of machine learning.

Explore Curriculum
Strategic AI Leadership — Frame AI as a business strategy, not a technology project.
Data Fluency — Read, question, and challenge data outputs with practitioner confidence.
Ethics & Governance — Navigate bias, fairness, and the regulatory frameworks shaping AI.
Generative AI — Move beyond ChatGPT to enterprise-grade LLM deployments.
Harvard Case Studies — Pedagogy drawn from real business problems, not textbooks.
Curriculum
8 Modules.
One Transformation.

A carefully sequenced programme — from building data vocabulary to deploying Generative AI — designed for leaders managing real organisations.

01
Foundation of Data Science
Develop the vocabulary to speak data with confidence across functions and seniority levels.
02
Data Visualization & Storytelling
Turn numbers into boardroom influence. Communicate findings that drive real decisions.
03
Predictive Analysis
Forecast outcomes that guide strategy — from demand planning to churn reduction.
04
Machine Learning with Business Applications
See algorithms in action on real business problems — without writing a single line of code.
05
Machine Learning using Python
Gain the practical fluency to engage with, review, and manage technical teams effectively.
06
Neural Networks & Deep Learning
Understand the architecture powering modern AI — from image recognition to large language models.
07
Reinforcement Learning
Lead systems that learn, adapt, and optimise in dynamic business environments.
08
Generative AI
Harness LLMs, ChatGPT, and enterprise AI agents to create genuine competitive advantage.
