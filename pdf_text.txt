IIMBxBrand Playbook  ·  v 2.0031.1 · THE PROMISE
THE BRAND, IN ONE SENTENCE
The same faculty who teach IIM Bangalore's flagship programmes will teach 
you. Live online, on -demand, and on the Bannerghatta campus when the 
programme calls for it.
That sentence is the entire brand. Everything else in this book is a consequence of it.
IIMBx is not edX. IIMBx is not Coursera. IIMBx is not a video library with a famous logo on top. The faculty who teach the country's most respected managers in residence at IIM 
Bangalore —they are the same faculty who teach the learner sitting at home in Pune at eight on a Tuesday evening. The same lect ures. The same case studies. The same 
standard.
The three modes —live online, on -demand, and on campus —are not a hierarchy. Most of the learning lands as on -demand video, re adings and assignments, because that is 
where the depth gets built. Live cohort sessions punctuate that work. Campus immersion at Bannerghatta exists where a programme genuinely benefits from being in a room 
together . None of the three is 'the real one'; the mix is the product.01  ·  The Story

IIMBxBrand Playbook  ·  v 2.0 051.3 · NON -NEGOTIABLES
Five principles. Every decision answers 
to them.
01 Faculty first, technology second. Every feature, claim, and design decision answers one question: does this make the faculty's teaching more powerful, 
or less?
02 Asynchronous by design, live where it earns its 
place.The product is overwhelmingly on -demand —that is where most of the depth is built. Live cohort sessions punctuate 
that work; they are not a substitute, and not the headline.
03 Campus is real, not metaphor. When we say campus immersion, we mean the actual Bannerghatta campus, with the actual faculty, in actual rooms. 
We never borrow the word.
04 Outcomes over enrolments. We measure ourselves on what learners do after they graduate —promotions, ventures, transitions —not on how 
many of them signed up.
05 Bengaluru, India, world. We are rooted here. The world will come to us when our work earns the visit.

IIMBxBrand Playbook  ·  v 2.0 082.5 · REVERSE USAGE
Approved —but only the canonical 
reverse.
THE RULE
The lockup has two approved applications: full colour on white, and the canonical reverse (parchment + crimson 
on charcoal). No third treatment is licensed.
Aclean reverse is the right answer when the surface is dark and a parchment plate would feel heavy. The reverse is canonical, not improvised —it is 
supplied as a separate artwork file, not generated on the fly with a CSS filter.
The parchment plate becomes the fallback for busy or photographic surfaces where neither full -colour -on-white nor reverse will read cleanly. Use it 
sparingly. Solid white -on-dark, monochrome navy, monochrome crimson —all unlicensed.02  ·  Logo System

Two applications. Use one of the two.
APPLICATION 01 · ON WHITE
The default. Certificates, formal academic correspondence, the LMS, the verification page, any product

IIMBxBrand Playbook  ·  v 2.0 1103 · COLOUR · MERIDIAN
Two registers. One system.
IIMBx wears two voices. The institutional voice is the voice we use when speaking on behalf of IIM Bangalore —ceremonial, formal, 
regulated. The sister -brand voice is the voice we use when IIMBx is being itself —marketing, social, product, learner -facing.
A learner who follows IIMBx through a year of marketing, enrolment, classes, and graduation should encounter the sister -brand voice 
50% of the time, and the institutional voice on the certificate at the end. That ratio is the brand's centre of gravity.03  ·  Colour · Meridian

IIMBxBrand Playbook  ·  v 2.0 1908 · MERCHANDISE
The X is a design element, not a logo.
The merchandise system is built on one principle: the X is not an applied logo, it is a design element. Every approved 
treatment does something with the X —scales it, splits it, repeats it, embeds it, hides it, hand -draws it.
Three creative engines power the system: the X as constellation (alumni map), the X as multiplier (you ×x), and the X 
as cultural object (the kolam). Recognising which engine a treatment uses is how the system stays coherent.08  ·  Merchandise · T -shirts

IIMBxBrand Playbook  ·  v 2.0 2309 · CO-BRANDING
Sister, not subsidiary.
IIMBx is the digital learning foundation of IIM Bangalore —a sister brand, not a subsidiary, not a sub -brand, not a product lin e. The 
relationship is structural and visible: the IIMB crest sits permanently inside the IIMBx lockup, and IIMBx never appears with outit. This 
is not a mark of dependence. It is a mark of inheritance.
When IIMBx and IIMB co -publish, the IIMBx lockup leads, the IIMB seal sits in the footer or in a co -presentation panel. When IIM B 
publishes alone, no IIMBx mark is required. When IIMBx publishes alone, the inheritance is already inside the lockup.
With third -party partners, the IIMBx lockup leads on IIMBx -led work. Partner marks sit adjacent —separated by a vertical hinge rule 
that mirrors the lockup's own internal hinge, at equal optical weight. We do not lock partner marks into our lockup.11 · EXECUTOR'S SUMMARY
The whole brand, on one page.
If you have five minutes before a vendor call, read only this.
THE PREMISE The same faculty who teach IIM -B's flagship programmes will teach you. Live online, on -demand, and on the Bannerghatta campus wh en the programme calls

THE PROMISE "The same faculty. Wherever you are." Eight words. Use it once per piece, never as a headline.
THE MARK Horizontal lockup is the default. Two applications: full colour on white, or canonical reverse on charcoal. X -only for favicons and embroidery.
THE COLOURS 50% parchment · 15% charcoal · 1 5% marigold · 20% crimson + navy. Crimson + navy on certificates and formal academic surfaces. Marigold on everything else.
THE FONTS Tiempos Headline (or Source Serif Pro) for display. Inter (or IBM Plex Sans) for body. IBM Plex Mono for hex codes and eyebro ws.
THE VOICE Bold like Nike. Warm like Airbnb. Strong verbs. Concrete nouns. Adjectives are the seasoning, not the meal. Address one perso n.
THE TWO VOICES Institutional (crimson + navy): certificates, convocation, formal launches, regulatory filings. Sister -brand (marigold + charcoa l + parchment): everything else.
